## Brand & Style

This design system expresses timeless luxury, architectural stature, and unwavering trust for premier event venues—from gilded banquet halls and heritage estates to state-of-the-art convention centers. It balances the grandeur of celebratory milestones with the rigorous utility demanded by event planners, property hosts, and platform administrators.

The visual direction merges **Minimalism** with **Tactile Modernism**:
- **Purity of Surface**: Generous negative space, crisp structural lines, and restrained ornament evoke white-glove hospitality.
- **Architectural Credibility**: Structural grids and balanced typography evoke high-end architectural monographs rather than transactional classifieds.
- **Dignified Trust**: High-value venue commitments require visual stability—solid surfaces, crystalline status indicators, and confident typographic hierarchy eliminate ambiguity.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an atmosphere of prestige, warmth, and verified legitimacy:

- **Royal Midnight Navy (`#0F172A`)**: The primary anchor. Delivers authoritative depth across display headings, core navigation, and primary interactive states. Replaces harsh blacks with deep architectural slate.
- **Warm Champagne Gold (`#B45309`, accenting to `#D97706` & `#FDE68A`)**: The secondary luxury signature. Employs antique brass and polished gold tones for featured listings, prime badges, interactive focal points, and curated ratings.
- **Verified Emerald (`#10B981`, deep shade `#065F46`)**: The tertiary validation accent. Applied exclusively to verified venue badges, confirmed slot locks, escrow deposits, and positive yield metrics.
- **Crisp Ivory Slate (`#F8FAFC`, stepping through `#F1F5F9` and `#E2E8F0`)**: The foundation neutrals. Creates warm, gallery-like surfaces that let photography shine without clinical sterility.
- **Alert & Status Tones**: Crimson Rose (`#E11D48`) for booked-out or urgent holds; Deep Amber (`#D97706`) for pending contracts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial elegance of **Playfair Display** with the clean, modern legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display)**: Used for venue titles, showcase statements, pricing amounts, and section headers. Conveys heritage, celebration, and architectural beauty.
- **System Interface & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Used for functional data, capacity metrics, contract schedules, search filters, and table components. Its open geometry guarantees legibility at small sizes.
- **Labels & Badges**: Capitalized or semi-bolded, tracking out slightly (+0.04em to +0.06em) to deliver a polished, structured feel in capacity and amenity chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system optimized for content-rich media and dense management dashboards:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column grid, 32px gutters, max-width bounded at 1360px with generous margins. Venue search views leverage a split-pane format (55% listings, 45% interactive map).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column grid, 24px gutters, collapsable multi-filter sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, 16px gutters, bottom sticky booking bar for venue reservation CTAs.
- **Dashboard Reflow (Owners & Admins)**: Fixed 260px collapsible dark-navy sidebar navigation with a responsive fluid workspace area utilizing 24px internal card padding.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids loud, ungrounded dropshadows, favoring architectural depth achieved through refined, warm-tinted ambient shadows and subtle borders:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Crisp ivory slate (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Modules)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and shadow `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Flyouts)**: Elevated white cards with a 1px border of `#CBD5E1` and ambient shadow `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Sticky Booking Bars & Modals)**: Floating white canvas with border `#CBD5E1` and ambient elevation `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
- **Gold Accent Glow (Featured / VIP Listings)**: Subtle rim illumination using `0 0 0 1px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.25), 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a **Soft (Level 1)** curvature system, evoking refined, tailored luxury rather than casual consumer roundness:

- **Base Inputs, Badges, and Small Buttons**: `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Listing Cards, Content Panels, and Tables (`rounded-lg`)**: `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Modals, Floating Booking Panels, and Hero Overlays (`rounded-xl`)**: `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Pills / Status Dots**: Retained solely for categorical micro-tags and verified check icons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep Royal Navy background (`#0F172A`), white text, subtle gold highlight on hover (`box-shadow: inset 0 -2px 0 #B45309`), smooth scale transition.
- **Luxury Gold Action (Reserve Now / Promoted)**: Warm Champagne Gold (`#B45309`), white bold text, transforms to `#92400E` on hover.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure white fill with 1px slate border (`#CBD5E1`), navy text, slate tint hover (`#F1F5F9`).

### High-Contrast Badges & Status Chips
- **Verified Venue**: Emerald deep background (`#ECFDF5`), emerald text (`#065F46`), accompanied by a sharp shield checkmark icon.
- **Air Conditioning (AC / Non-AC)**: Navy outline badge (`#0F172A`) with ice-blue tinted interior (`#F0F9FF`) and crisp typographic distinction.
- **Guest Capacity**: Minimalist pill showing icon + range (e.g., `⚲ 500 – 1,200 Guests`) in neutral slate (`#334155`) with `#F1F5F9` backdrop.
- **Availability State**:
  - *Instant Bookable*: Crisp ivory fill, emerald border, emerald indicator dot.
  - *Under Hold*: Soft amber tone (`#FFFBEB`), amber text (`#92400E`).
  - *Sold Out*: Soft slate fill (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#64748B`), strike-through line.

### Cards & Listings
- **Venue Showcase Card**: High-definition 16:9 imagery with top badges (Verified, Prime, Capacity), heart wishlist icon on glassmorphic disc. Lower compartment features Playfair Display title, neighborhood label, starting rate per plate/day, and split secondary metrics (Floating vs. Seated capacity, Parking slots).
- **Subtle Outline**: Never borderless; always defined by 1px `#E2E8F0` border to maintain form structure against pure ivory backgrounds.

### Search & Management Filters
- **Universal Booking Bar**: Floating dock with 4 compartmentalized inputs: Location, Event Date, Guest Count, and Venue Type. Active compartment gains a 1px Champagne Gold border.
- **Owner & Admin Tables**: Crisp, high-density tabular view with sticky headers, quick status-toggle dropdowns, slot blackout calendar triggers, and payout confirmation triggers.

### Form Inputs & Date Range Selectors
- **Input Fields**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, transition to a 2px `#0F172A` ring on focus with gold indicator caret.
- **Date Range / Shift Selector (Morning, Evening, Full Day)**: Segmented control switches with clear visual states and real-time slot conflict warning cues.
